### 🎓 Who am I?

I am a Dutch **Computer Scientist (MSc)** specialising in the intersection of **Artificial Intelligence**, **Machine Learning**, and **Software Engineering**. I hold a Master's degree in Computer Science with a specialisation in Artificial Intelligence from TU Delft.

* **🔬 Research Interests:** Deep Generative Modelling, Probabilistic Machine Learning, Normative Modelling, Explainable AI, AI for Healthcare, and Sustainable Machine Learning (Green AI).
* **🧠 Broader Scientific Interests:** Artificial General Intelligence (AGI), Computational Neuroscience, Artificial Life, and Predictive Modelling.
* **⚙️ Engineering Focus:** Software Architecture, DevOps, MLOps, (Green) Software Engineering, System Design, Data Science, and Applied AI.

My goal is to translate complex and challenging problems across different domains into reliable models, software, and systems, bridging the gap between scientific research and practical applications.

* **Research Experience:** I completed my Master's at **TU Delft**, where my thesis project, [*VAE-based Multivariate Normative Modeling: An Investigation of Covariate Modeling Methods*](https://repository.tudelft.nl/record/uuid:b042ba6a-6fc9-4c12-ad86-69387c7a831e), was conducted in collaboration with **Erasmus MC**, using data from the [Generation R Study](https://generationr.nl/researchers/) and the Healthy Brain Network. My research focused on using deep generative models (VAEs) to characterise individual deviations in brain measures while investigating methods for accommodating covariates such as age, sex, and site-related batch effects in clinical datasets. This involved developing an open-source research framework and systematically evaluating ten covariate-modelling methods across approximately 10,900 structural MRI observations. Previously, I conducted undergraduate research at the **Delft Blockchain Lab**, investigating privacy-preserving technologies in blockchain-based self-sovereign identity systems.

* **Engineering Experience:** I've built a wide variety of systems, ranging from interactive science communication software for [Project HERSENSPIN](https://www.erasmusmc.nl/en/sophia/research/projects/hersenspin) at Erasmus MC, to the backend and scalable server infrastructure for [HauntedMC](https://github.com/HauntedMC), an online multiplayer gaming network that I founded and developed to support over 1,000 concurrent players. My work also includes research software such as the [Multivariate Normative Modeling Kit](https://github.com/remdui/MultivariateNormativeModeling), and [GATorch](https://github.com/GreenAITorch/GATorch), a PyTorch extension for monitoring and analysing energy consumption during deep learning experiments. My latest project is [Mylonite](https://github.com/remdui/Mylonite): a self-hosted portfolio website solution.

* **The "Toolbelt":** I've ended up working with a pretty wide range of languages, frameworks, and research tools over the years. My primary languages include Python, Java, C++, R, and Bash, alongside frameworks such as PyTorch, TensorFlow, and scikit-learn. This diversity has made me less attached to any single "favourite" tool and more focused on picking whatever makes the most sense for the project at hand.

* **Systems & Infrastructure:** I also have a professional background in systems and network administration, including infrastructure modernisation, network services, and system security. I tend to think about the full lifecycle of what I build, so I'm used to handling infrastructure, containerisation, deployment, and making sure things actually stay running. In research, this also means paying attention to reproducibility, computational efficiency, and systematic experimental evaluation.
